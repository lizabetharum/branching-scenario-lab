AI role-play simulation · Prototype

Pharmacy Coaching Role-Play

A working prototype built in Next.js on the Anthropic API. Pharmacy managers coach an AI technician who knows why a performance problem happened and shares each fact only when the manager asks a good question.

  • Leading questions or a handed-over fix make the technician more guarded. Open questions and recovered missteps open the conversation back up.
  • Five defined failure states, from mistakes the learner can repair to full system failure.
  • Feedback quotes the manager's own words and gives one next step per skill. Learners can flag any label the AI assigns.
  • Code controls transitions and scoring. Layered guardrails block personal information, clinical questions, off-topic chat, and prompt injection.
  • A release gate of 23 risk-based test cases runs before every deploy.

Try this: start with a leading question, then switch to an open one, and compare what the technician tells you.
